About TC Cards

TC Cards is a small, free, unofficial way to carry the Table Conversations questions for evenings when the box isn’t with you. The questions are by Practicing the Way, shown word for word. TC Cards is not affiliated with or endorsed by Practicing the Way.

Put it on your home screen

iPhone: in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Open it once from the icon while you’re online and unlock it there. After that it works without signal.

Android: in Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu, then Install app (or Add to Home screen).

At the table

Slide or flick a card to the left, as its writing reads, to set it aside and turn up the next. Let go before it’s across and it settles back.

To turn a card for the person opposite, twist it round with two fingers, as you would a real card. Either of you can twist it back. The buttons do the same.

Privacy

Nothing leaves this phone. TC Cards stores only which card numbers you’ve drawn, your theme and tick setting, and the unlocked key. No accounts, cookies or analytics.
